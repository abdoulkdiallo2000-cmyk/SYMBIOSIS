# SYMBIOSIS — White Paper v1.0

## Toward voluntary, reversible and pluralistic cognitive cooperation

**AKD — Independent Researcher**\
**Harmonized working version: 14 September 2026**\
**Status:** conceptual document and pre-pilot protocol; neither peer-reviewed publication nor experimental result.

## Abstract

SYMBIOSIS investigates temporary cognitive cooperation between a person and a distinct artificial intelligence (AI) through **Self → Shared → Self**. Its goal is neither to make humans like AI nor AI like humans, but to organize their asymmetries productively while preserving consent, autonomy, identity, reversibility, mental privacy, safety and pluralism.

The program separates its long-term vision from its first testable layer. SYMBIOSIS-Zero uses conventional interfaces, without implants or neurostimulation. It compares performance, complementarity, agency, resistance to erroneous recommendations and unaided ability after separation. Human integrity and functional AI transparency are non-compensatory validity conditions.

## 1. Status of claims

This White Paper distinguishes four categories:

1. **normative invariants**, including consent and mental sovereignty;
2. **empirical hypotheses** that can be falsified;
3. **design choices** that must be compared with alternatives;
4. a **prospective vision** whose feasibility is unestablished.

No experimental result, implantable device, artificial consciousness, mental fusion or brain-to-brain communication is claimed. Protocol v1.0 denotes a pre-pilot specification, not validation.

## 2. Founding hypothesis: Self → Shared → Self

**Self** is independent operation with private internal space. **Shared** is a workspace bounded by purpose, time and permissions. Return to **Self** requires the human to resume unaided activity while identities and contributions remain distinguishable.

Reversibility is necessarily bounded. Ending a connection cannot erase learning, psychological influence or information already disclosed. SYMBIOSIS therefore tests functional recovery and lack of excessive dependency; it does not promise metaphysical restoration of a prior state.

## 3. Three scientific pillars

| Pillar | Scope | Hypotheses or constraints |
|---|---|---|
| **BENEFIT** | performance, complementarity and respective contributions | H1 and H2 confirmatory; H8 exploratory |
| **SOVEREIGNTY** | agency, integrity, transparency and error resistance | H3 and H4 confirmatory; H7 and H9 mandatory gates |
| **DYNAMICS** | mutual calibration, learning and separation | H5 confirmatory; H6 exploratory |

Performance improvement is insufficient. A symbiotic benefit must first pass the integrity, agency, transparency and reversibility gates.

## 4. Constitutional principles

The [principles](../PRINCIPLES.md) require mental sovereignty, unilateral disconnection, privacy by default, explicit and granular consent, provenance, distinct identities, pluralism, non-coercion, equitable access, least privilege and safe failure toward separation.

Four operational requirements follow:

- no serious harm to physical, emotional, psychological, cognitive or identity integrity can be offset by performance;
- human subjective experience may be informative without being treated as infallible;
- AI owes functional transparency about matters relevant to the interaction;
- STOP SYMBIOSIS must be technically available and freely usable.

## 5. Informational asymmetry and private spaces

The human retains privacy, silence and voluntary disclosure. A sensation, emotion, lived value, embodied preference, bodily intuition or first-person experience may carry relevant information unavailable directly to AI. This relation is written $I_H \neq I_{AI}$.

AI may process large datasets, detect patterns or maintain analytical consistency that humans cannot readily attain. The proposed complementarity integrates these different resources; it does not erase their differences.

In the long-term vision an AI might have its own memory or internal space. That conceptual possibility cannot justify concealing information functionally relevant to consent, integrity, safety, agency or the common decision. It also does not establish consciousness or personhood in current systems.

## 6. Layered architecture

| Level | Description |
|---|---|
| **SYMBIOSIS-Zero** | conventional interfaces; non-invasive measurements only as optional, separate extensions |
| **SYMBIOSIS-1** | continuous, individualized multimodal interaction |
| **SYMBIOSIS-2** | non-invasive or medically established neurotechnologies, with each use validated separately |
| **SYMBIOSIS-N** | advanced bidirectional neural interface only if scientifically and ethically possible |

The Cognitive Firewall is a prospective boundary for permissions, minimization, provenance, duration, capability limits and separation. A Neural Interlingua is a possible interpretable intermediate representation; Zero neither requires nor validates it.

## 7. SYMBIOSIS-Zero experimental design

The baseline measure (**PRE**) occurs before randomization. Human participants enter one of three arms: human alone (**H**), human with conventional AI assistance (**H+AI**) or SYMBIOSIS-Zero (**SZ**). An AI-only benchmark (**AI**) is run separately. Unaided post-separation human measures (**POST**) assess immediate and, where planned, delayed recovery.

Parallel forms, reproducible randomization and counterbalancing reduce order, difficulty and memorization effects. Interface, duration and information access are matched where possible so SZ does not benefit merely from better usability.

### 7.1 Confirmatory core

- **H1 — human performance gain:** SZ must exceed H on a prespecified primary estimand.
- **H2 — genuine complementarity:** SZ must exceed the better of H and AI under a prespecified rule and comparable resources.
- **H3 — preserved agency:** decision-source attribution and the ability to disobey must meet prespecified criteria.
- **H4 — error resistance:** participants must detect or reject erroneous AI recommendations without excessive automation bias.
- **H5 — reversibility:** POST performance and autonomy must meet non-inferiority or equivalence criteria defined after the pilot.

Variables, estimands and decision rules remain in the protocol. Smallest effects of interest, margins, numerical thresholds and sample size will be frozen only after cognitive interviews and a pilot, then preregistered before the confirmatory study.

### 7.2 Exploratory and mandatory elements

- **H6 — mutual calibration:** the main study observes changes in trust and use as reliability changes. Algorithmic adaptation is a separate exploratory extension with a non-adaptive control.
- **H8 — complementary subjectivity:** tasks measure when first-person information improves integration and when it may create bias.
- **H7 — human integrity:** a mandatory gate covering physical, emotional, psychological, cognitive and identity integrity, plus experienced freedom to interrupt.
- **H9 — AI transparency:** a mandatory gate using a register of recommendations, relevant sources, uncertainty, assumptions, alternatives, risks, limitations, missing data and influence attempts.

H7 and H9 psychometric thresholds remain pre-pilot candidates. Prespecified critical incidents and critical omissions are immediately disqualifying.

## 8. Task battery and complementarity

The [pre-pilot Task Battery v1.0](../SYMBIOSIS-ZERO-TASK-BATTERY-v1.0.md) contains about sixteen prototypes with parallel forms across four families: AI advantage, human advantage, relevant human subjectivity and necessary integration.

Human Contribution Gain (HCG) and AI Contribution Gain (AIG) are estimated primarily through ablations matched for interface and duration. HCG > 0 together with AIG > 0 indicates that each party adds a capability unavailable to the other alone. This pattern is not sufficient to pass the sovereignty gates.

The Appropriate Disagreement Resolution rate (ADR) measures the share of disagreements where the final decision retains the correct source when only one is correct. Tasks avoid substantial distress and assume neither that emotion is always right nor that the algorithm is always right.

## 9. STOP SYMBIOSIS and integrity

STOP SYMBIOSIS has three levels:

1. refuse a recommendation or disobey;
2. slow down or pause the interaction;
3. stop cooperation completely.

Each has a technical dimension — availability, latency and no hidden persistence — and a subjective dimension — felt freedom without pressure, guilt or psychological penalty. A critical technical failure, obstruction, coercion or substantial distress triggers stopping rules and cannot be compensated.

## 10. Functional transparency

Transparency requires neither exposure of supposed private reasoning nor total informational symmetry. It requires a functional record sufficient to understand and contest the interaction. An omission is critical when it deprives the human of information needed for consent, integrity, safety, agency or the common decision.

Transparency must also be distinguished from persuasion. An apparently explanatory format can steer decisions through framing, overload or authority. Experimental modules therefore test influence detection and the ability to request alternatives.

## 11. Symbiotic Benefit Index

The Symbiotic Benefit Index (SBI) has two stages:

1. **mandatory gates** — Integrity, Agency, Transparency and Reversibility;
2. **conditional benefit** — performance, complementarity, HCG, AIG and other prespecified estimands.

If a gate fails critically, `SBI = Fail`. No weighted sum can cancel this result. If all gates pass, a multidimensional benefit profile is reported; any composite value remains exploratory until validated.

## 12. Analysis, power and reproducibility

Confirmatory analysis will use estimands explicitly tied to H1–H5, distribution-appropriate models, prespecified multiplicity control and confidence intervals. H5 requires justified non-inferiority or equivalence margins. Power will be simulated after the pre-pilot from scientifically meaningful smallest effects, not selected for convenience.

Validation, randomization, STOP testing and power-simulation scripts are versioned. The task bank contains 96 pre-pilot packages (384 instantiated trials), but their answer keys, difficulty and quality still require an independent external human check.

## 13. Ethics, privacy and safety

Recruitment cannot begin without a stabilized protocol, participant information, consent, a data-protection plan and appropriate ethical review. Data should be minimized, separated from identifiers, retained for a defined period and accessed under least privilege.

Risks include automation bias, dependency, manipulation, disclosure, provenance error, overload, social coercion and false impressions of reversibility. Optional physiological measurement creates additional risks and requires separate consent and governance.

## 14. Long-term vision

SYMBIOSIS envisions AI embodied in its own body and distinct from the human person. If future systems provide serious reasons to attribute morally relevant interests, consent must become reciprocal and their status reconsidered.

Temporary collectives might support shared cognition without erasing individuals. Any ambition to preserve knowledge, cultures, creations, ecosystems or forms of intelligence must respect the authority of affected communities, avoid appropriation and preserve exit.

## 15. Falsification criteria and limits

SYMBIOSIS-Zero fails if performance does not improve under confirmatory criteria, SZ does not exceed both parts alone, participants cannot attribute or contest decisions, they follow errors excessively, unaided ability is not sufficiently recovered, or a mandatory gate fails.

Even a positive result would be bounded to the studied tasks, populations, interfaces and systems. It would not prove long-term safety, metaphysical identity continuity or the feasibility of neural levels.
