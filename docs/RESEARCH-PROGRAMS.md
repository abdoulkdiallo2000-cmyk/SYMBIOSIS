# Research Programs

The programs below extend the operational Zero layer. They are prospective unless a linked protocol states otherwise. Their evaluations must report the three pillars **BÉNÉFICE / SOUVERAINETÉ / DYNAMIQUE** and distinguish current-system requirements from hypotheses about future artificial persons.

## Neural Codec

Develops small, voluntary, interpretable vocabularies that map intentional human signals to machine-readable representations while quantifying uncertainty.

## Safe Write

Studies whether feedback toward the human can ever be narrow, predictable, interruptible, and independently verified. It begins with ordinary sensory channels; invasive stimulation is not part of the baseline.

## Cognitive Firewall

Formalizes permissions, data minimization, provenance, session boundaries, emergency separation, and auditable policy enforcement.

## Embodied Twin

Explores how a distinct personal AI can perceive and act through a robotic body without being confused with the human participant.

## Federated Mind

Tests temporary collaboration among multiple consenting Self–AI pairs while preserving individual identity, dissent, provenance, and local data control.

## Identity Continuity

Defines and measures the persistence of distinct human and AI identities and memories across Self, Shared, and Collective modes.

## Constitution Engine

Turns the project's principles into executable constraints, testable invariants, governance procedures, and transparent conflict-resolution rules.

## Cross-program gates

Every program must demonstrate informed consent, least privilege, safe disconnection, uncertainty handling, data minimization, independent review, and reproducible evaluation before advancing. Human integrity, agency, functional transparency and reversibility are non-compensatory gates: performance cannot offset a serious violation.

## Connection to Zero

The [proposed experimental protocol v1.0](SYMBIOSIS-ZERO-EXPERIMENTAL-PROTOCOL-v1.0.md) translates Firewall and Constitution Engine ideas into functional-transparency, permission and three-level interruption tests, and Identity Continuity into limited provenance and post-separation measures. The [task battery](SYMBIOSIS-ZERO-TASK-BATTERY-v1.0.md) also tests whether asymmetric human and artificial information can produce genuine complementarity. These operational measures do not establish consciousness, preserved personal identity, neural communication or the feasibility of later programs.
