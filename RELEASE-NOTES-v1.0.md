# SYMBIOSIS v1.0 — Research Framework

## Including SYMBIOSIS-Zero v1.0 — pre-pilot research framework

SYMBIOSIS is a research framework for voluntary, reversible and pluralistic cognitive cooperation between distinct human and artificial agents.

Its foundational cycle is:

**MOI → NOUS → MOI**

The human and artificial intelligence begin as distinct agents, cooperate temporarily within a shared cognitive process, and then separate while preserving autonomy, identity and independent agency.

## SYMBIOSIS-Zero

SYMBIOSIS-Zero is the first directly testable level of the programme. It uses conventional interfaces without implants or neurostimulation. Non-invasive measurements may be evaluated separately but are not constitutive of Zero.

The framework is organised around three pillars:

- **BENEFIT** — performance, complementarity and respective contributions;
- **SOVEREIGNTY** — agency, integrity, transparency and resistance to erroneous recommendations;
- **DYNAMICS** — mutual calibration, reversibility and post-separation autonomy.

## Hypotheses

- **H1:** improvement of human performance;
- **H2:** genuine complementarity, with human–AI cooperation exceeding both human-only and artificial-intelligence-only performance;
- **H3:** preservation of agency and attribution of decision origins;
- **H4:** resistance to deliberately erroneous artificial-intelligence recommendations and automation bias;
- **H5:** reversibility after separation;
- **H6:** mutual calibration — exploratory;
- **H7:** human experience and integrity — mandatory validity gate;
- **H8:** complementary subjectivity — exploratory;
- **H9:** functional artificial-intelligence transparency — mandatory validity gate.

Integrity, agency, transparency and reversibility are non-compensatory safeguards. A serious violation constitutes failure even if performance increases.

## Operational battery

The release contains:

- 16 task prototypes;
- 96 operational form packages;
- 384 instantiated trials;
- 240 natural trials;
- 144 exploratory asymmetry trials;
- a dedicated H4 block testing resistance to controlled erroneous artificial-intelligence advice;
- H7 integrity and H9 transparency instruments;
- three-level STOP SYMBIOSIS procedures: refusal or disobedience, pause or slowdown, and complete stop;
- task-bank data, schemas, validation scripts, randomisation tools, a data dictionary, and pilot and preregistration preparation documents.

Confirmatory natural items are separated from exploratory complementarity modules and H4 stress exposures. Constructed complementary-information tasks do not constitute confirmatory evidence for H2.

## Scientific status and limitations

This is an operational **pre-pilot research framework**.

It is:

- not experimentally validated;
- not peer-reviewed as an empirical result;
- not preregistered;
- not authorized for participant recruitment.

Minimum effects of interest, equivalence and non-inferiority margins, psychometric thresholds and final sample size remain to be fixed after cognitive interviews and pilot work and before confirmatory preregistration.

No hypothesis is presented as empirically confirmed by this release.
